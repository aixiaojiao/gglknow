THE GGLKNOW GUIDE

Make your saved ideas useful.

From X to your local reader, then into your own knowledge base. Everything you need to get started.

Start saving

  1. Open the extension popup and enter a subfolder such as GglKnow. Paths are relative to Chrome’s default download directory. Do not enter an absolute path or repeat Downloads.
  2. Choose HTML, JSON, Markdown, or a combination. Enable image downloads if needed, then save your settings.
  3. Manually expand the post or open the article on X. Once the content has loaded, click Collect. The extension does not expand, scroll, or paginate for you.

Read and organize

  1. Open the reader from the popup and select the complete saved folder, including HTML/JSON files and the media subfolder. Multiple formats of the same post are deduplicated.
  2. Search text, authors, or tags. Filter by author, tag, collection, images, or threads. Add your own tags and collections.
  3. Folder access is temporary. Select your folder again after reopening or refreshing the reader; tags and collections stay in the browser.
  4. Use the sidebar to switch between English and Simplified Chinese. Source content, custom tags, and collection names are preserved. The popup and X capture controls follow Chrome’s interface language.

Bring it into Obsidian

  1. Move the Markdown file and its sibling media folder into your Obsidian vault together. Keep their relative positions so images resolve.
  2. Properties include title, type, source, author, author_handle, url, created, captured, published, tags, and capture_scope. Field names and source content are not translated.
  3. After adding tags in the reader, export Markdown again. Its image paths still refer to the original saved media folder.

What gets saved

  1. Only content currently loaded on the page is saved. Unloaded, collapsed, or virtualized-away content is excluded. A saved thread may be incomplete.
  2. Saves share a five-second interval across tabs, with one task at a time: up to 25 loaded posts, 50 images, and two concurrent image downloads.
  3. This version has no video downloads, AI tagging, paid subscriptions, or developer cloud storage. Follow X’s rules and respect the rights of content owners.

Troubleshooting

  1. Capture stopped working after an update: refresh the X page and try again. Avoid repeated clicks.
  2. Images are missing: reimport the complete saved folder, including media. If an image download fails, text may still be saved and a partial-success notice appears.
  3. Old metadata cannot be read: export a backup first. The reader preserves unrecognized existing data and prevents overwriting it. Both legacy backup wrapper formats are supported.
  4. Privacy: saved files and reader metadata stay local. Chrome may sync extension settings when browser sync is enabled. Image downloads contact X’s image servers; data is not sent to the developer.
